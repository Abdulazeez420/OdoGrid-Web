Description of Service

OdoGrid is a comprehensive, offline-first vehicle management application for Android. It helps users track and maintain their vehicles with the following core capabilities:

  • Fuel and charging logs with efficiency calculations (MPG, L/100 km, km/L, kWh/km)
  • Maintenance records and smart date / odometer-based reminders
  • Expense tracking with category breakdown and cost-per-km insights
  • Business and personal trip logs for tax mileage deduction reports
  • AI receipt scanning (OCR) via on-device Google ML Kit
  • Multi-format export — PDF, Excel, JSON
  • Encrypted local storage with optional Google Drive and Dropbox cloud backup
  • Support for multiple vehicle types including petrol, diesel, hybrid, EV, motorcycle, truck, and more

Some features are gated behind a short rewarded ad or a one-time in-app purchase. All core logging and tracking features are available at no cost.
